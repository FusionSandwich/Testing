# Prompt 4 approach registry

Statuses in this registry are exactly `ACTIVE`, `BLOCKED`, `REJECTED`, or
`PROVED`.  A route becomes `PROVED` only after ordinary proof, adversarial
audit, current-API integration, deterministic regression, and the applicable
Lean boundary agree.

| Mechanism | Acceptance question | Initial status | Kill or completion condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| differentiated cotangent partial fractions | Is the `csc^2` expansion termwise justified with a positive, uniformly bounded tail on `0<x<=pi/4`? | ACTIVE | reject a merely formal series; prove explicit `1/80` tail and transferred rate/quality constants |
| direct asymmetric polar solve | Do three coordinate equations uniquely force `a_v,a_+,a_-` without symmetry, reversibility, or equal masses? | ACTIVE | reject any proof that assumes `a_+=a_-` |
| fixed-graph minimax | Does the local lower bound combine with the verified reversible construction to give the exact infimum over the stated graph class? | ACTIVE | reject fitted examples or a lower bound without attainment |
| universal and quasi-uniform moment bounds | Do normal balance and Cauchy--Schwarz give every displayed constant exactly? | ACTIVE | complete self-contained algebra and transfer normalization |
| constrained finite extremal compactness | Are separation, covering, degree, locality, mass, equilibrium, symmetry, and rate-cap constraints all closed and uniformly bounded? | ACTIVE | reject an unconstrained or noncompact class |
| feasible-cone projectivization | Is `Q` optimized over conductance weights rather than treated as geometry-only? | ACTIVE | prove the projective bijection and exact variance identity |
| sliced LP and dual certificate | Are signs and normalization transferred correctly at fixed mean loss? | ACTIVE | require primal/dual equality, certificate, equality case, and sharp examples |
| reduced-ring incidence | Is the obstruction limited to biregular/perfect-matching adjacent-ring couplings? | ACTIVE | reject any general split/merge impossibility claim |
| Lean finite core | Does the port compile on the verified P3 API without axioms or placeholders? | ACTIVE | require accepted declaration map and focused axiom audit |

| wholesale old-branch import | Can old P1--P3 replacements preserve the verified baseline? | REJECTED | forbidden by ancestry and path policy |

## Adversarial cases kept live

The independent audits must test asymmetric polar rates, unequal masses,
nonreversible local rows, formal-series remainders, collapsed/dense/nonlocal
extremal sequences, conductance reweighting, sampling aliases, positive graph
curvature, split/merge ring couplings, old-commit ancestry contamination, and
mutable read-only branch movement.

## Boundary corrections from adversarial audit

- The nonzero tangent-balanced cone modulo scaling is projective; the
  fixed-normal-moment family is an affine slice and is bijective, without a
  scaling quotient, to the balanced probability polytope.
- For arbitrary normal moment `lambda`, the invariant quality is
  `Q_lambda=r*epsilon/lambda^2`. It agrees with the spherical `Q=r*epsilon/4`
  at `lambda=2`.
- The sliced minimum is stated only for a nonempty balanced polytope.
- Opposite rays alone do not force half weights. The unqualified route is
  `REJECTED`; the accepted route records the tangent-magnitude ratio.
