# Spherical positive feasibility and shared-edge duality

The complete ordinary proof is split into two reviewable parts:

1. [`SPHERICAL_LOCAL_FEASIBILITY.md`](SPHERICAL_LOCAL_FEASIBILITY.md) — exact
   non-antipodal feasibility, indexed relative-interior strictness, uniqueness,
   antipodal classification, cone margin, rate bounds, conditioning, and
   perturbation constants.
2. [`SHARED_EDGE_DUALITY.md`](SHARED_EDGE_DUALITY.md) — edge-column cone,
   feasible polytope, weighted centering, complete-graph construction, full
   Farkas alternative, strong LP duality and complementary slackness, strict
   feasibility, sensitivity, sparse reconciliation, exact examples, and the
   prior-art boundary.

Together these two files are the authoritative ordinary proof for this stage.
The theorem-to-file map is in `../docs/THEOREM_TO_FILE_MAP.md`; exact rational
regressions are under `examples/`.
