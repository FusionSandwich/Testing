# Primary-source prior art and corrected novelty statement

This release makes no literature-priority or journal-acceptance claim. The
comparison below uses the primary papers already recorded in
`docs/publication_program/p1f_manuscript/priority_sources.bib` and the detailed
hypothesis-transfer audit. A source's theorem is not imported into AFP without
its hypotheses, sign, mass, normalization, and sampling convention.

## Corrected novelty statement

The defensible contribution is the sharp, alias-correct product frontier

`D_2 r_max >= d(d-1)`

for finite positive reversible coordinate-exact spherical generators,
together with its exact covariance decomposition, equality/global-compatibility
conditions, weighted near-extremizer budget, and justified extremal/matching
families. The release does not claim to introduce positive spherical
Laplacians, graphical designs, tight frames, convex eigenpair preservation,
positive local stencils, or angular Fokker--Planck discretization itself.

## Numerical-transport boundary

Prior angular Fokker--Planck, BFP, discrete-ordinates, adjoint, meshfree, and
graph-sparsifier work is acknowledged in the existing P2A/P2B primary-source
matrix. P2A's scoped distinction is the fixed generalized-mass,
shared-conductance, alias-correct finite certificate problem. P2B's scoped
distinction is the explicit conversion of that residual into a guarded
six-channel error framework. Neither is advertised as the first AFP method,
the first convex graph design, or a physical transport improvement.

An external specialist must still check completeness of the bibliography and
priority assessment before submission.
