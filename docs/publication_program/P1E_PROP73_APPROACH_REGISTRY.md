# Proposition 7.3 repair approach registry

## Decision

**OUTCOME B SELECTED.** The universal derivative route is blocked on the live
repository evidence because the literal expression DAG absent from the
purported certificate is indispensable. The strongest theorem justified by
the existing unperturbed construction is fixed-level local persistence with an
existential, level-dependent radius.

The registry is organized by mathematical mechanism. A route is not promoted
because another route failed, because a script exits successfully, or because
a large constant was proposed.

## Mutation obligations

The independent verifier
`p1e_fixed_level_persistence_audit.py` rejects all of the following contract
mutations:

1. coordinate-eigenvalue sign reversal;
2. deletion of the one-half mass factor;
3. insertion of a prescribed perturbation power;
4. promotion to a radius uniform in the level;
5. removal of the guarded denominator domain;
6. a singular unperturbed local system;
7. removal of the positive transition-gap hypothesis;
8. alteration of a frozen transition row or mask;
9. removal of reflection;
10. loss of one-shared-conductance-per-edge normalization;
11. support-graph changes; and
12. a nonpositive radius.

These mutations test the theorem contract and repository scope. They are not a
substitute for the ordinary continuity proof.

## Final mechanism boundary

The accepted proof uses

\[
 \mathcal U_J=\{\eta:\det A_q(\eta)\ne0\ \forall q,
                    \ \Gamma_e(\eta)>0\ \forall e\}
\]

as an open set containing the unperturbed point. It then chooses a positive
sup-norm ball in `\mathcal U_J`. This is exact at each fixed level. A future
all-level theorem must add a genuinely new uniform certificate mechanism; it
cannot be recovered by citing the size of the rejected constant, passing CI,
or sampling more levels.
